# Commercial Terms

## Early Access

- Early access costs US$12 per named developer per month and is month-to-month.
- Subscribers receive priority consideration for requested fixes, but no fix, delivery date, or outcome is guaranteed.
- Early-access purchases provide downloadable builds containing completed fixes before those fixes are added to the public release.
- You only pay for months when early-release fixes are available. No fixes? Your subscription will be credited.
- Early-access builds may also contain other unreleased fixes, so early access is not exclusive to a single patch.
- Early-release fixes become eligible for public release at least 30 days after first customer delivery. Fixes that require substantial investment may remain in early access longer.

- Additional warranties, support terms, SLA commitments, or expanded liability coverage are available only by separate written agreement.
